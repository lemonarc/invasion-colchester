
<h2>Grand Total for 2026</h2>
<br/><br/>

<br/><br/>

<p>
    What an incredible result! <strong>Invasion Colchester 2026
    raised a phenomenal £23,526 for charity!</strong>
</p><p>
    This fantastic total includes donations collected during
    Invasion Colchester itself, our hugely successful
    <strong>Prize Draw</strong>, and fundraising activities held
    throughout the year — including <strong>Chris's Film, TV
    &amp; Music Quiz</strong> nights, contributions from local
    businesses such as <strong>MP Gallery</strong> and
    <strong>Red Lion Books</strong>, and the wonderful
    <strong>Maldon Grinch</strong>, whose fundraising went
    directly to the <strong>Multiple Sclerosis Trust</strong>.
</p><p>
    Our heartfelt thanks go to all the local businesses,
    sponsors, venues, organisations and individuals who
    supported Invasion Colchester 2026. Whether you donated
    money, provided prizes, organised a fundraiser, hosted
    an activity or helped make the event possible, your
    generosity has made a real difference.
</p><p>
    Special thanks go to our charity partners —
    <strong>St Helena Hospice</strong>,
    <strong>Colchester &amp; Ipswich Hospitals Charity</strong>
    and <strong>Mind (Mid &amp; North East Essex)</strong> —
    and to everyone who helped raise funds for these
    important local causes.
</p><p>
    Thanks also to our incredible crew, volunteers, costumers,
    special guests, performers, artists, exhibitors, vehicle
    owners, groups and societies. Your time, enthusiasm and
    dedication are what make Invasion Colchester such a
    special event.
</p><p>
    And to everyone who joined us in 2026 — whether you
    dressed up, bought a prize draw ticket, attended a quiz,
    supported a local fundraiser or simply dropped a donation
    into a collection bucket — <strong>THANK YOU!</strong>
</p><p>
    Behind all the costumes, characters, cars and entertainment
    is a community coming together to make a difference.
    <strong>£23,526 is an achievement we can all be proud of!</strong>
</p>
